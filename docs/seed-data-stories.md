# Seed Data Stories

12 employees.

3 major projects:
- **Atlas** — API platform migration (re-architecting the core backend services)
- **Beacon** — Customer-facing dashboard (new analytics product for end users)
- **Cipher** — Auth & permissions overhaul (security hardening and SSO integration)

Overall structure:
- Most engineers submit 5 PRs per week
- Most PRs get 2-3 comments
- Most engineers have 3-4 meetings per week, including one team meeting with the full team
- Engagement surveys have an 80% response rate

PR approvals: Alice approves Atlas PRs, Bob approves Beacon PRs, Frank self-merges Cipher weeks 1-2 / James approves weeks 3-4.
Survey skips (~2/week): Iris skips most weeks, David skips week 4, others occasionally.

## Alice Nakamura — Backend Tech Lead — Atlas
- Weeks 1-2: Heads-down on Atlas. Only has 1-1s with Henry and Karen. Doesn't comment or review PRs outside the trio.
- Week 3: Atlas ships. Starts attending broader team meetings again.
- Week 4: Re-engaging — reviewing PRs across the team, mood is high.

## Bob Torres — Frontend Tech Lead — Beacon
- Weeks 1-2: Helpful reviewer, manageable load. Reviews 3-4 PRs/week, ships 1-2 of his own. Good mood.
- Week 3: Review load increases as Beacon ramps up. Own PR output drops. Slight mood dip.
- Week 4: Bottleneck — reviewing 6+ PRs/week, own output stops entirely. Blocker mentions being stretched thin.
